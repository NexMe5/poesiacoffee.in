Roasted our own way to a medium dark profile, the last stage before dark. It's the sweet spot for those who find light roasts too subtle and dark roasts too intense, equally suited to espresso and black coffee.

Equipment & expertise

Great coffee depends as much on the equipment as it does on the bean.

A curated portfolio built for hospitality: traditional espresso machines, fully automatic systems and precision grinders, each selected to work in harmony. We help you choose the right setup, then support it with training, cup after cup, service after service.

Astoria Tanya R

Reliable, simple and professional: a timeless espresso machine built for demanding coffee environments, with precision pressure monitoring for consistent extraction.

  • Up to 3 groups
  • Copper boiler
  • AEP & SAE versions
  • Auto water refill

CIME Eera

A modern professional espresso machine with intelligent dose programming and an intuitive display. Elegant Italian design with reliable, durable metal construction.

  • Integrated shot timer
  • Steel steam wands
  • Auto cleaning

Eureka Motion 65

A professional on-demand grinder for precision and everyday reliability, with a touchscreen display and a hands-free portafilter fork.

  • 65 mm flat burrs
  • Stepless adjustment
